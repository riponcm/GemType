Welcome to GemType

Free grammar checking and rewriting on any website, with your own Gemini key. Three quick steps.

1

Add your free Gemini API key

Open aistudio.google.com/apikey, sign in with Google, click Create API key (no credit card), and paste it here. It stays on this device.

2

Check your writing when you want

GClick the GemType badge in the corner of any text box.
Ctrl Shift SpaceOr press the shortcut while you type.
FixImproveEnglishSelect text to fix, rewrite or translate it.
3

Pick how often GemType checks

You can change this any time from the GemType button in your toolbar.

Safari: click the GemType button next to the address bar and choose Always Allow on Every Website, so GemType can check your writing on any site.
